## Combined limits

**32 conservative process-start/attempt slots:** 10 helper/test, 2 trial roots, 6 wrapper/tool/controller allowances, 8 checks/cleanup/closeout and 6 reserve. Charge all hosts, launchers, interpreters, tests, tools, failed attempts and observed extra processes to one shared ledger; snapshots must detect any observed count exceeding the reserved allowance and stop. Reserve before launch and protect eight closeout slots. No previous meter changes or automatic new allocation.

**15 minutes preparation, 5 minutes execution**, **10 seconds per child**, **512 MiB private commit per controller/child**, **>=4 GiB available RAM**, **one child at a time**, **16 MiB new disk**, **1 MiB aggregate output**, **zero downloads/external requests/Ollama calls**. Controller Job permits only itself plus the one current direct child; worker Job remains active-process limit one, kill-on-close and no breakaway. No relaxation to accommodate an unexplained process. Read-only post-wait snapshots are bounded within the same time/output budget and do not authorize another worker.
